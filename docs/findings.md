# Nationwide LYBALVI Prescribing Findings

Scope: CMS Medicare Part D, service years 2023–2024,
50 states and Washington, DC.

## 1. Observed LYBALVI claims increased by 45.9%

Claims increased from 39,134 in 2023 to 57,090 in 2024.
The number of NPIs with published LYBALVI records increased
from 1,623 to 2,344.

Claims include refills and do not represent unique patients.
A larger observed prescriber count does not establish new adoption.

## 2. Growth occurred among continuing observed prescribers,
with a larger contribution from changes in record visibility

The 1,132 NPIs observed in both years increased their claims
from 31,457 to 37,363: a gain of 5,906, or 18.8%.

Newly observed records contributed 19,727 claims.
No-longer-observed records contributed -7,677 claims.

Reconciliation: 5,906 + 19,727 - 7,677 = 17,956.

CMS excludes provider–drug records with fewer than 11 claims.
Appearance or disappearance therefore does not establish
the start or cessation of prescribing.

## 3. LYBALVI share increased within the defined comparison set

Observed selected-set share rose from approximately 0.274%
to 0.382%, an increase of 0.108 percentage points.

The denominator includes LYBALVI and the exact generic-labeled
products Olanzapine, Aripiprazole, and Quetiapine Fumarate.
This is not total market share or an oral-only comparison.

## Interpretation

These findings describe historical public reporting patterns.
They do not establish marketing causality, patient suitability,
manufacturer revenue, or sales potential.

## Validation

558,954 source records passed the pipeline checks.
Two cached-input reruns produced identical CSVs.
Duplicate records, missing required columns, and negative claims
were rejected without changing valid exports.
